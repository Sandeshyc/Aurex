The Club

The strength floor

Built for lifting. Squat racks and platforms, a full run of free weights, and machines for the work you want isolated.

  • Racks and lifting platforms
  • Dumbbells and barbells
  • Plate-loaded and pin-stack machines

The cardio deck

A dedicated space for conditioning, from easy warm-ups to hard intervals, kept apart from the lifting floor.

  • Treadmills
  • Bikes and rowers
  • Stair climbers
  • Cross-trainers
