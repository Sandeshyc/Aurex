# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS: one folder, no build step, opens in any browser.

## Users

People in Bangalore choosing a gym: working professionals and regular lifters comparing premium gyms near home or office, deciding whether to visit, try a session, or join.

## Product Purpose

Aurex Club is a premium gym in Bangalore offering strength training and cardio (a full, well-equipped floor rather than a single-discipline box). The website's job is to make Aurex feel unlike every other premium gym in the city and turn a visitor into a walk-in, trial booking, or membership enquiry.

## Positioning

A premium all-round gym in Bangalore: strength and cardio under one roof, at the top end of the market. The site presents it at the craft level of the best premium gym websites (see Brand Commitments). No unique mechanism beyond that has been stated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Training offered: strength workouts, cardio workouts. Other offerings (classes, personal training, recovery, nutrition) are **undecided**; do not present them as fact.
- Location: Bangalore. Exact address, hours, and phone are **undecided**.
- Pricing and membership tiers are **undecided**.
- The free first session ("Your first session is on us") is shown on the site but is **pending owner confirmation**.
- Online trial booking is not connected yet; main.js CONFIG takes a form endpoint or WhatsApp number.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Aurex Club**.
- The user first asked for a "crazy looking" website, then rejected a concept-led design (a running-track world) as "too bad" on 2026-11-09 and asked for a site in the style of the best premium gym websites in India.
- Standing preference: convention-led premium gym design, executed at full craft. Reference bar: Equinox (luxury club craft: dark, full-bleed photography, refined uppercase type, minimal copy) and Anytime Fitness India (Indian conventions: "try us free" up front, simple plans, local details).
- Photography: high-quality stock gym photos (Unsplash) until the club supplies its own; the user confirmed this.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No photos, trainers, testimonials, member counts, pricing, or press. Future work must not fabricate these as real; any sample content is labelled as placeholder and listed for the owner to replace.

## Product Principles

1. Look like it belongs alongside the best premium gyms in India, through craft and restraint rather than gimmicks.
2. Get the visitor to act: visit, book a trial, or enquire.
3. Never claim what the club has not confirmed.
